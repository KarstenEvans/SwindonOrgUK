## Premise
ToomorrowMan and AI-PI encounter a cabinet that appears ordinary until one drawer reveals an item which perhaps belongs to a different century, and another reveals an object whose purpose nobody has yet quite managed to agree upon. The narrator introduces each *actual item photo* supplied by the owner; the humorous fictional adventure may embellish its journey, while an easy-to-read fact card tells what is known and links original sources. Schrödinger the tuxedo cat may investigate, with predictable problems for the cabinet's internal dimensions. The actual physical cabinet comes first, story treatment later.
