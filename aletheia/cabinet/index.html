What is the Cabinet of Curiosities?

It is Aletheia's creative reading room: a central index for original animated histories, interactive stories, experiments and unusual discoveries, with links to their actual published pages.

Are the stories factual?

Some entries investigate real-world history and link to their sources; others are clearly imaginative fiction or experiments. Each publication should explain which is which.

What about books, practical guides and downloads?

Those belong principally to the companion Aletheia Emporium, which is being planned as a separate practical collection. Until it is ready, our existing Swindon.org.uk Resources and Aletheia Knowledge remain accessible.

Can a physical cabinet become part of the story?

Yes, once the owner has supplied photographs. The planned ToomorrowMan adventure can introduce each photographed object with a separate researched fact card and fictional scene. No physical contents are assumed here.
