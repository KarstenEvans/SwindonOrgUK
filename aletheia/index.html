What makes Aletheia different?

We aim to separate what is evidenced from what is suggested or imaginary. Our worldwide discovery work will use one adaptable place-and-language interface, rather than publishing a duplicate site for each city. Creative fiction remains labelled as fiction, and sources should lead to the original publication.

The current site is a static discovery front door. It does not claim to run an automatic live AI research engine, publish a city newsletter or automatically verify today's events.
