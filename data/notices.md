# SwindonOrgUK community notices

Human-editable source for small current notices and recurring/community events shown on What's On and, where relevant, Meet.

## Editing rules

- Keep newest/current items near the top.
- Verify venue, date, time and public link before publication.
- Remove or archive expired one-off notices.
- Do not place passwords, private invitation tokens or personal contact details here.
- Reusable app logic belongs in its own project. This file holds changing public event/notices data.

## Suggested notice format

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — Event title

**Time:** 18:30–21:00  
**Venue:** Public venue / Online  
**Type:** Dance | Book Club | Chess | Community | Other  
**Status:** Confirmed | Verify  
**Link:** https://example.org/

Short useful description.
```
