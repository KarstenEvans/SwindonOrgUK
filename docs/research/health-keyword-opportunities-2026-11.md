# UK Health Search Topics — scoping audit (10 November 2026)

**Purpose:** Editorial prioritisation only. **Not** proof of exact UK Google rankings or Search Console performance. No Search Console, Google Ads Keyword Planner, Google Trends exported data or licensed SEO account was connected for this run. Search-demand figures below are externally reported estimates and should not be called first-party Google analytics.

## User-requested evergreen clusters
- Diabetes → type 1/type 2, glucose/food, foot safety, medication education.
- Fasting → intermittent fasting and safety in diabetes.
- Supplements → vitamin D, vitamin B12, protein, creatine, magnesium, probiotics; claims.
- Movement → stretching, calisthenics, Tai Chi, strength and balance.
- Back and neck → lumbar pain, sciatica, Modic changes, neck stiffness.
- Lower limb → hips, legs, ankles, feet, swelling and neuropathy warning signs.
- Ageing → strength, balance and healthy longevity rather than unsupported anti-ageing claims.
- Gut microbiome → antibiotics, prebiotics (inulin, FOS, GOS), SCFAs, butyrate; uncertainty.

## Third-party UK demand signals
- Independent Pharmacy, UK diet keyword analysis (published March 2025, reviewed March 2026): reported monthly searches include **keto diet 162,000**, **intermittent fasting 128,000**, **Mediterranean diet 51,000**, **low-carb diet 38,000**; methodology is their own, not our Search Console. https://www.theindependentpharmacy.co.uk/weight-loss/guides/uk-most-googled-diets
- Vitgem UK supplement keyword market report (updated 2 Sept 2026; licensed data and their own demand index, NOT click totals): high-demand terms include **creatine**, **magnesium glycinate**, **protein powder**, **vitamin B12**, **probiotics**, **vitamin D** and **apple cider vinegar**. The demand index must not be misreported as an exact Google monthly search count. https://vitgem.co.uk/pages/uk-gummy-search-report
- Google Trends public UK trending page supports exploration but is not a comprehensive most-searched health ranking: https://trends.google.co.uk/trending?geo=GB

## Recommendation
Pilot 4–6 high-value pages and measure real performance in Search Console after launch. Prefer usefulness, source strength and internal links over pumping out hundreds of keyword variants. Follow the site GUI's answer-first content model. Priorities: Type 2 diabetes, fasting, supplements, back pain, gut microbiome and health claims. Specific supplement topics should follow quality review, not commission.

## Medical evidence and conflicts needing further research
- BMJ AIM randomized trial on Modic changes: three-month amoxicillin did not produce clinically important benefit; the prior positive trial and low-grade `Cutibacterium acnes` hypothesis do not justify routine antibiotic treatment. https://www.bmj.com/content/367/bmj.l5654.long
- Antibiotic use and diabetes meta-analysis describes association, not proof of causality nor an established doxycycline-specific causal effect. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34622400/
- Prebiotic fibre may alter microbiome/SCFA outputs, but responses vary; vinegar is not an established microbiome reset. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41579-024-01108-z
- UK food/supplement marketing must meet authorised health-claims rules. https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/15.html
