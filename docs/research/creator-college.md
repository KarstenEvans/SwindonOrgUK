## 4. What we can legally/safely link to

### Good pattern

For material Creator College/Jun has genuinely published publicly:

- link to the specific public page or video;
- name/credit Creator College or the named creator;
- explain why the link is useful;
- use YouTube's official embed player if the uploader has embedding enabled;
- consider YouTube privacy-enhanced (`youtube-nocookie.com`) embeds on Swindon.org.uk;
- avoid implying Creator College endorses Aletheia or Swindon.org.uk.

### Important distinction

**Credit does not turn copying into permission.** UK government copyright guidance says simple links to material publicly posted by the rights holder are usually not restricted by copyright, but copying and hosting protected images/content yourself will usually require permission. Do not use links designed to bypass paywalls or membership controls.

UK guidance: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/copyright-notice-digital-images-photographs-and-the-internet/copyright-notice-digital-images-photographs-and-the-internet

YouTube explicitly supports sharing and embedding through its official player:

- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780
- https://uk.youtube.com/t/terms

### Do not do

- do not copy/rehost paid course videos;
- do not reproduce their downloadable templates or course PDFs;
- do not scrape/paywall-bypass protected lessons;
- do not copy their graphic identity or testimonials wholesale;
- do not link to pirated course mirrors;
- do not present their frameworks as Aletheia inventions.

## 5. What Aletheia should borrow as a pattern, not content

| Creator College mechanic | Aletheia adaptation |
|---|---|
| Start with a person's "why" | Ask what the user actually wants AI to help with |
| Clear finished outputs | Every guide should produce something useful: memory file, site, audit, CV, search result, etc. |
| Short step-by-step lessons | Prefer 2-5 minute clips and concise written instructions |
| Templates/resources | Provide original Aletheia templates under our own licensing |
| Live audit/feedback | AI self-check + optional human review, with evidence and approval boundaries |
| Community/accountability | Optional community links only, never required for the app to work |
| Platform-specific teaching | Provider adapters around a vendor-neutral Aletheia core |
| Monetisation alongside learning | Free core, disclosed affiliate/resource links outside the canonical logic |
| Visual radial "why" diagram | Cleaner Aletheia/Swindon roundabout navigation, while keeping original identity |

## 6. Swindon.org.uk use

Recommended external-content model:

1. Add a curated **Learn / Creator / AI resources** section only where it helps an existing task.
2. Link to specific useful free Creator College or YouTube items rather than a generic homepage when possible.
3. Put a one-sentence Aletheia note before the link: what it teaches and what to verify.
4. Keep the Swindon page useful if the external resource disappears.
5. Run a link checker periodically.
6. Mark affiliate links separately if Creator College later restores/approves an affiliate programme.

Do not turn Swindon.org.uk into a Creator College mirror.

## 9. Research conclusion

The main value is not cloning Creator College. It is recognising several packaging ideas that fit Aletheia:

- start from the user's goal;
- make each lesson produce something;
- keep steps short;
- link to excellent public material instead of reproducing it;
- separate free utility from commercial links;
- verify claims even when a page looks polished.

Aletheia's distinctive angle remains portability, inspectable memory, source/trust discipline, local usefulness, user choice and a free/distributed architecture.
