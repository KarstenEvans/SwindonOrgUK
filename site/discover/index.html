How does Discover work?

Can I search outside Swindon?

Yes. Change the place field to any town, region or country. Your selected destination does not need to match where you are physically located.

Does Aletheia verify every place automatically?

Not in this introductory page. It prepares independent web and map searches, plus a source-checking brief for a web-enabled AI. Check the original source for opening times, events, prices and dates. Search snippets are not verified reports.

Can I receive the answer in another language?

The research brief requests your chosen answer language, while asking the receiving AI to retain original local-language names and source links. This page's own interface is currently in English.

Is it free?

Discover and its source links are free to use. External services may have their own terms or account limits. Aletheia presents independently sourced information separately from any optional, disclosed commercial resources.
