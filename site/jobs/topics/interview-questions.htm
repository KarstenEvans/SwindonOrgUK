Interview questions: prepare evidence, not scripts

Prepare examples of decisions, challenges and results using a concise situation-action-result structure; tailor evidence to the job.

Practical steps

  • Read the job description and identify essential skills.
  • Prepare credible, measurable examples and questions about the role.
  • Practise answers aloud without memorising a rigid script.

Check: Check each employer's current process and use original evidence, not invented credentials or roles.
