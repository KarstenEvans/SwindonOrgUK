LinkedIn profile: position yourself for senior roles

A senior-level LinkedIn profile should communicate the problems you solve, the scale of your work and accountable outcomes.

Practical steps

  • Use a headline that reflects the next role and your strongest specialist value.
  • Write an About section with responsibilities, business outcomes and relevant technical/leadership experience.
  • Verify every claim, keyword and achievement; tailor without overstating your experience.

Check: Check each employer's current process and use original evidence, not invented credentials or roles.
