How to research a prospective employer

Use official company information, current news and job descriptions to test whether the role, team and company are a fit.

Practical steps

  • Start with the company's own site, careers page and filings where relevant.
  • Cross-check independent news, public product documentation and project history.
  • Prepare one or two specific questions informed by research; don't present rumours as fact.

Check: Check each employer's current process and use original evidence, not invented credentials or roles.
