Intermittent fasting: benefits and risks

Intermittent fasting changes when people eat; it may help some people control energy intake or weight, but results vary and it is not suitable for everyone.

What matters

  • Common approaches include time-restricted eating and alternate-day variations; there is no single best plan for all people.
  • People taking insulin or medicines that can cause low glucose should seek medication advice before fasting.
  • A balanced diet, hydration and a sustainable routine matter more than a fashionable fasting label.
Important: Pregnancy, eating disorder history, frailty and some medical treatments require individual clinical advice before fasting.
