Diabetes: type 1 and type 2

Diabetes means blood glucose is too high, but type 1 and type 2 have different causes and treatments.

Choose your diabetes guide

What matters

  • Type 1 is an autoimmune condition. Insulin is essential; dietary changes do not replace it.
  • Type 2 involves insulin resistance and/or insufficient insulin. Management may include food, activity, medicines and glucose monitoring.
  • Unexplained thirst, frequent urination, fatigue or weight loss warrant prompt assessment. New symptoms with vomiting, confusion or rapid breathing need emergency assessment.
Important: Do not assume a person's diabetes type from age or body weight.
