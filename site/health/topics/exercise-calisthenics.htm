Movement · Help Topic

Stretching and calisthenics at home

Simple, comfortable movements and body-weight exercises can support flexibility, strength and balance, without expensive equipment.

What matters

  • Start at a level that matches current mobility and choose a stable support for standing exercises.
  • Slowly increase repetitions and include movements for strength, balance and flexibility rather than aggressive stretching.
  • Stop activity that makes pain worse or causes dizziness, numbness or new weakness; seek professional assessment for concerning symptoms.
Important: Anyone with recent injury, neurological symptoms or unexplained significant pain should obtain tailored physiotherapy/clinical advice.
