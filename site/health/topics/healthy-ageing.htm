Healthy ageing: strength, balance and function

Healthy ageing is less about a miracle compound and more about maintaining strength, mobility, social connection and management of preventable risks.

What matters

  • Regular activity supports cardiovascular health, balance and muscle function.
  • For many older adults, weekly strength, flexibility and balance practice matters alongside aerobic activity.
  • Claims about biological-age reversal, longevity supplements or anti-ageing treatments require higher-quality human outcome evidence.
Important: Match activity to ability, and discuss significant new symptoms or major programme changes with a clinician.
