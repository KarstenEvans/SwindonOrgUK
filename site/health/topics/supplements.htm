Supplements: what is worth checking?

Supplements can correct specific shortfalls, but many marketed benefits are unproven and excessive doses can cause harm.

What matters

  • In the UK, adults are advised to consider 10 micrograms of vitamin D daily in autumn and winter; some groups need it year-round.
  • Vitamin B12, iron or other nutrients may need assessment where deficiency risk or symptoms exist.
  • A claimed benefit is not the same as a verified clinical outcome. Check dose, interactions, quality and authorised marketing claims.
Important: Do not use supplements instead of prescribed treatment. Ask a pharmacist about possible interactions.
