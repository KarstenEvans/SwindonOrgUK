Type 1 diabetes: an essential guide

Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition in which the body cannot produce enough insulin; regular insulin treatment is essential.

What matters

  • Symptoms can develop quickly: needing to urinate, thirst, tiredness and unexpected weight loss.
  • Daily insulin, glucose monitoring and education in food/activity effects are core parts of management.
  • Diabetic ketoacidosis can become life-threatening. Vomiting, abdominal pain, deep breathing or confusion with diabetes symptoms call for emergency help.
Important: Fasting or supplements must never replace insulin; discuss major diet changes with the specialist diabetes team.
