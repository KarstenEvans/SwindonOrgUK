Lumbar back pain and sciatica

Lower-back pain can arise from several structures, and sciatica describes pain or neurological symptoms related to nerve irritation.

What matters

  • Stay as active as reasonably possible; avoid prolonged bed rest for uncomplicated episodes.
  • A clinician can assess whether self-management, physiotherapy or specialist review is appropriate.
  • Imaging is not routinely needed for every episode; severe or progressive neurological signs change the urgency.
Important: Seek emergency help for new bladder/bowel difficulties, saddle numbness or severe/progressive weakness, especially with bilateral symptoms.
