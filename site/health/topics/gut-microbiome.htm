Gut microbiome: fibre, inulin, GOS and butyrate

Dietary fibres feed different gut microbes. Fermentation can produce short-chain fatty acids, including acetate, propionate and butyrate, but effects vary between people.

What matters

  • Prebiotic fibres include inulin, fructo-oligosaccharides and galacto-oligosaccharides (GOS). Food sources include leeks, onions, beans and some artichokes.
  • Increasing fibre too quickly can cause gas and bloating; smaller portions and gradual increases are often better tolerated.
  • Butyrate is one product of fermentation, not proof that a specific supplement prevents or cures disease. Research outcomes are variable.
Important: Vinegar contains acetic acid but is not an established substitute for dietary fibre or a proven way to restore the microbiome after antibiotics.
