Modic changes: inflammation, bacteria and antibiotics

Modic changes are MRI findings at vertebral endplates. A low-grade bacterial explanation has been proposed for some cases but remains contested.

What matters

  • Type 1 changes generally reflect bone-marrow oedema/inflammatory signal; type 2 changes predominantly fatty marrow signal.
  • Cutibacterium acnes, a common skin bacterium, has been studied as one possible contributor, but biopsy results and causation are disputed.
  • The larger double-blind AIM trial did not find a clinically important benefit from three months of antibiotics for chronic low-back pain with Modic changes.
Important: Having acne or groin boils does not establish a spinal infection. Groin lesions can instead be hidradenitis suppurativa, which requires its own assessment. Do not self-treat Modic changes with doxycycline.
