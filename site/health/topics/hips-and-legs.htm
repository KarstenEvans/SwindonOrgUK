Hip and leg pain: common causes and warning signs

Hip and leg pain can involve joints, tendons, muscles, circulation or nerves; an individual examination matters more than guessing from location.

What matters

  • Hip stiffness and pain with walking can have several causes, including osteoarthritis or tendon problems.
  • Pain radiating from the back may be nerve-related; swollen or hot joints raise different concerns.
  • Balance and strengthening can help some mechanical problems, but should match the diagnosis and symptoms.
Important: A hot swollen hip with fever needs urgent assessment; a severe injury with inability to bear weight needs emergency assessment.
