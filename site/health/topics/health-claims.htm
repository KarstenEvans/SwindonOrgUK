How to check a health claim

A convincing mechanism, attractive graph or testimonial does not prove a product meaningfully improves health.

What matters

  • Find the exact claim, relevant population, measured outcome and quality of supporting human evidence.
  • Check whether the claim is about symptoms, a laboratory marker, risk reduction or an actual clinical outcome.
  • Look for independent replication, conflicts of interest, adverse effects and whether marketing wording is legally authorised.
Important: Articles can discuss developing research without converting it into a product advertisement or implying supplements treat disease.
