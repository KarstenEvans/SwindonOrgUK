Foot and ankle pain: swelling, balance and diabetes

Foot or ankle pain often has a mechanical cause, but new warmth, redness, swelling or loss of sensation can need prompt assessment.

What matters

  • Insoles, suitable shoes, gentle mobility and pacing may help some mechanical problems after assessment.
  • Changes in arch shape, weakness or numbness need assessment rather than a generic exercise plan.
  • People with diabetes and foot pain or swelling should seek timely professional help.
Important: A hot swollen foot, rapidly worsening pain, fever, inability to walk or new colour change requires urgent assessment. Do not assume infection or Charcot without proper evaluation.
