Neck pain and stiffness

Neck stiffness is often temporary, although persistent or worsening pain and neurological symptoms should be examined.

What matters

  • Gentle movement and simple comfort measures can help uncomplicated neck pain.
  • Avoid unsafe driving or cycling if you cannot rotate your head properly.
  • New arm weakness, spreading numbness, gait changes or serious injury warrant medical assessment.
Important: Avoid forcing the neck through painful ranges. Seek urgent care when neck pain follows major trauma or accompanies serious neurological symptoms.
