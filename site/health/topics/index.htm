Diabetes

Diabetes: type 1 and type 2

Diabetes means blood glucose is too high, but type 1 and type 2 have different causes and treatments.

Open topic ↗

Type 1 diabetes: an essential guide

Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition in which the body cannot produce enough insulin; regular insulin treatment is essential.

Type 2 diabetes: what helps?

Type 2 diabetes involves insulin resistance and/or reduced insulin production; management is individual and may include lifestyle change and medicines.

Nutrition

Intermittent fasting: benefits and risks

Intermittent fasting changes when people eat; it may help some people control energy intake or weight, but results vary and it is not suitable for everyone.

Supplements: what is worth checking?

Supplements can correct specific shortfalls, but many marketed benefits are unproven and excessive doses can cause harm.
