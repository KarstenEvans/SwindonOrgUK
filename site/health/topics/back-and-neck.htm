Back and neck pain: where to start

Most back and neck pain is non-specific and often improves, but persistent, severe or neurological symptoms need assessment.

What matters

  • Gentle activity and adapting daily tasks often help uncomplicated back pain.
  • Possible causes include muscular strain, joint or disc problems and sometimes nerve irritation; symptoms alone rarely pinpoint one tissue.
  • New bladder or bowel dysfunction, numbness around the genitals or bottom, or severe progressive leg weakness are emergency warning signs.
Important: High fever, rapidly worsening pain or feeling seriously unwell with back pain can signal infection: seek urgent assessment.
