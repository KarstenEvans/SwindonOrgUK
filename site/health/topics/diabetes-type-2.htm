Type 2 diabetes: what helps?

Type 2 diabetes involves insulin resistance and/or reduced insulin production; management is individual and may include lifestyle change and medicines.

What matters

  • The NHS emphasises healthy eating, movement, weight management when appropriate, and prescribed medication.
  • Complication prevention includes blood pressure, cholesterol, kidney, eye and foot checks.
  • Remission is possible for some people, but it is not a universal cure and does not justify stopping prescribed medicine independently.
Important: Seek prompt advice for new foot pain, swelling, ulcers or rapidly worsening glucose-related symptoms.
