Tai Chi: gentle movement and balance

Tai Chi uses slow, controlled movements and attention to posture and balance; it may provide an accessible route into regular physical activity.

What matters

  • It can form part of strength, balance and flexibility activity, particularly for older adults.
  • Choose a suitable beginner class and adapt standing positions to personal ability.
  • Progress gradually and use nearby stable support if unsteady; activities should not increase pain.
Important: Claims that Tai Chi cures medical conditions should be treated cautiously. Evidence and individual results vary.
