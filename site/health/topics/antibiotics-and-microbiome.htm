Antibiotics, gut bacteria and diabetes claims

Antibiotics can disturb gut microbes, but it has not been established that doxycycline itself causes type 2 diabetes.

What matters

  • Observational studies have found associations between antibiotic exposure and subsequent diabetes, but these cannot prove the antibiotic caused the condition.
  • Doxycycline is a tetracycline-class antibiotic, but medicines in the same class may differ in effects and exposure; avoid assuming identical risks.
  • There is no validated prescription of extra fibre, vinegar, GOS, protein or butyrate that reliably prevents antibiotic-associated metabolic effects.
Important: Never stop, avoid or self-start an antibiotic based on a microbiome hypothesis. Antibiotics can be essential for treating serious bacterial infections.
