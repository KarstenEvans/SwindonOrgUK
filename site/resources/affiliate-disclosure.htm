Affiliate Disclosure

Swindon.org.uk is an independent local guide. Some resource pages contain advertising or affiliate links that can help support the site.

In plain English: if you follow a link that we identify as advertising or an affiliate link and complete a qualifying purchase or action, Swindon.org.uk can receive a commission from the retailer, advertiser or affiliate network.

How we mark advertising

Where a page contains affiliate advertising, we aim to make that commercial relationship clear before or beside the relevant links. We use clear wording such as AD and explain that commission can be earned, rather than expecting visitors to understand the word “affiliate” on its own.

Networks and programmes

Affiliate networks and programmes used by Swindon.org.uk may include Awin, Bookshop.org and other programmes identified on the relevant resource page.

Different programmes use different tracking systems. A link that belongs to one programme may be kept separate from another network so that the correct attribution is preserved.

Awin automatic link conversion

Swindon.org.uk may use Awin's Publisher MasterTag and Convert-a-Link technology on pages that contain eligible commercial links. Convert-a-Link can turn an ordinary advertiser link into a tracked Awin affiliate link.

Links that need to retain attribution to another programme can be excluded from Awin automatic conversion.

Tracking and privacy

When you follow an affiliate link, the retailer, advertiser or affiliate network may process information needed to record the referral and attribute a qualifying transaction. The exact technology depends on the provider and its consent and privacy settings.

For Swindon.org.uk privacy information, see Privacy.

Editorial independence

An affiliate relationship does not buy favourable coverage on Swindon.org.uk. We may choose useful resources independently, and a commercial relationship should be disclosed where it applies.

Questions

If you have a question about a commercial or affiliate link on Swindon.org.uk, please use the Contact page.

This disclosure may be updated when the site's affiliate programmes or tracking methods change.
