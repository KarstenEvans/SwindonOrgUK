Aletheia Trust Check

Is this company, website, product or claim trustworthy? Prepare a source-based investigation with your chosen AI. The page prepares the research; the AI must verify actual sources before giving a verdict.

What would you like to check?

Optional location and extra context

Only include information you are comfortable pasting into an external AI. Avoid passwords, bank details and private messages.

This is a free, provider-neutral handoff. It does not run a live background search or access private Reddit/Discord content.

Open selected AI in a new tab
What is checked? Identity, official registers and warnings, independent reviews, repeating complaints, public Reddit/social discussions, suspicious marketing, and specialist evidence such as Microsoft technical advisories or clinical trials when relevant. A “scam” search hit is a lead, not proof.

How the verdict works

Trust Check uses reassuring, mixed, caution or insufficient evidence, with a separate high/medium/low confidence level. It should cite sources, show what was not checked, consider positive and negative evidence, and suggest further checks before you risk money or personal data.

The original method and updated rules are maintained as portable Markdown on GitHub after approval and merge. This page is not a guarantee, official rating or substitute for specialist advice.
